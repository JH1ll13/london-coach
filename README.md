# The London Coach

An installable web app for learning the London System in chess. You play White and Coach Lou grades every move, explains the ideas, and highlights recommended moves on the board to teach notation. Mistakes from your games come back later as puzzles on a spaced-review schedule.

**Play:** https://jh1ll13.github.io/london-coach/

Open the link on your phone and choose **Add to Home Screen** (iPhone, Safari) or **Install app** (Android, Chrome). After one visit online, it works offline.

Features: graded moves (green → red), coach explanations, 16 London puzzles, 6 lessons, game review with an evaluation graph, and spaced repetition. Progress is saved in the browser on each device.

The built-in engine runs in your browser. When online, the app may also load [Stockfish.js](https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js) (GPLv3) from a CDN for deeper analysis.
